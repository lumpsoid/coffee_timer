End of brew

A step sound marks one boundary of many; the finish marks the only end. So each candidate is longer, rings out, and resolves — a falling cadence, a slowing rhythm, a strike let ring — instead of one knock. All of them ship in Settings > Sounds > End of brew; they are generated by scripts/gen-sounds.py. “After a step” plays the step sound, a pause, then the finish, as at the end of a real brew.
