Brew page: the label, opened up

The brew page takes on the recipe's own label: its paper colour, texture and inks fill the screen, so opening Espresso feels like picking up the espresso bag. The timer becomes a dial with second ticks like a scale bezel, plus an outer track that shows the whole brew step by step. The phones below run a sped-up brew. Tap a dial to start or reset it.

Details

At a glance

  • The page colour says which recipe you're brewing.
  • The big number is the only thing you need mid-brew: time left in the step.
  • The outer track shows how far into the whole brew you are.

Interaction (unchanged)

  • Tap the dial to start, tap again to reset.
  • Long-press a step to start from it; the hint at the bottom says so.
  • The edit button stays in the app bar.

Open questions

  • Should the outer track and ratio ship now or later? The page works without them.
  • A Hero transition from the list card's badge into the page header would tie the two screens together.
