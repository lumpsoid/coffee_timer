Recipe list: coffee-bag labels

Each recipe is a printed label. Its tone (crema, caramel or roast) comes from total brew time: short brews look dark like espresso, long brews look light like filter coffee. The content is the same as today; only the look changes.

Espresso and Pour Over are the seeded recipes. AeroPress and French Press are made-up examples to show all three tones (coffee and water follow the existing 10 g / 50 g per step).

Details for a longer look

At a glance

  • The name is a stamped, spaced-out title you read first.
  • The tone colour tells recipes apart before you read anything.
  • Time is the one figure in accent colour.

On a closer look

  • The roast meter (three beans, top right) matches the tone.
  • Paper grain, a printed inner frame and notched ticket corners.
  • A faint coffee-ring stain, in a different spot on alternate cards.
  • The description reads as a tagline between dashes.

In Flutter

  • Notched shape: a ShapeBorder/ClipPath; frame, grain and stain: one CustomPainter, no image assets.
  • Fonts: Oswald + Fraunces bundled under assets/fonts (both OFL).
  • Label tones are separate from the accent colour; the app bar keeps the accent.
